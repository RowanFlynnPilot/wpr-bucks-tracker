## What it is

**The Bucks, by the numbers** — Wausau Pilot & Review's live Milwaukee Bucks
hub: live scores on game nights, the Eastern Conference race, the schedule with
TV listings, season stat leaders, and a film room that replays every game
(win-probability chart, shot chart, the big runs). It lives on
wausaupilotandreview.com and updates itself from ESPN's public data all season —
82 games plus the postseason, October through (hopefully) June.

Wisconsin sports fans check scores daily. This puts a WPR-branded scoreboard in
front of them every time — and your name rides along.

## Why it works

- **Season-long frequency.** A tracker isn't an article — readers come back to
  it all winter. NBA seasons run 82 games; that's 82 game nights of checking.
- **Local halo.** WPR is where Wausau looks first for news; the sponsorship
  reads as supporting local journalism, not as a banner ad.
- **Measurable.** Views, tab opens, and your clicks, straight from Plausible —
  shared monthly: ▢ page views/mo · ▢ uniques/mo · ▢ game-night peaks.
